# Breathe — Privacy Policy

_Effective 8 October 2026_

Breathe is a guided breathing app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you
use it. In short: **Breathe does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.** Everything the
app records stays on your device.

## What the app stores on your device

To show your progress and remember your preferences, Breathe saves the following in the app's
private storage on your phone:

- Your settings (sound, haptics, screen-awake, motion, guide style and session length)
- A log of the breathing sessions you complete (date, time, pattern and duration)
- How you felt after a session, if you choose to answer
- Your streak and the badges you have earned
- Your daily reminder days and time, if you turn reminders on

This information never leaves your device through Breathe. The app has no account, no server, no
analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting services, and it does not request internet access.

## Backup and device transfer

If you have turned on Android's backup, Android may include Breathe's stored data in your device
backup (for example, to your Google account) and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is
handled by Android under your device settings, not by Breathe, and the developer cannot access it.
You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.

## Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, Breathe asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are
scheduled and shown entirely on your device.

## Sharing

Breathe can open Android's share sheet so you can share your streak or a badge as a short text
message. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app and send it yourself, and the text goes only to
the app you pick.

## Deleting your data

You can delete everything Breathe has stored by clearing the app's storage (Settings → Apps →
Breathe → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

## Children

Breathe does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
